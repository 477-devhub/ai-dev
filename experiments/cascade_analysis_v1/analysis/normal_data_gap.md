## 3. Search for normal data inside the project (no download, no API)

Searched: `downloads/fight` (AI Hub 171 fight), `data/recovered` (AI Hub 171 swoon and trespass), `downloads/multiview_fight` (AI Hub 71953 fight, 5 events × 2 views), `evaluation_selection/`, `organized_data/` and `benchmark_v1/` (read only).

### The only in-project candidate: pre-event portions of the AI Hub 171 videos

The source videos run about 5 minutes. The annotated event starts late (median 118 s for fight, 242 s for swoon, 260 s for trespass), so the part before the event could supply candidate benign windows.

Windows counted as floor((event_start − 10 s guard) / 35 s):

| Source | Videos | Videos with ≥ 1 window | Nominal 35 s windows | Unique source scenes | Locally playable |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| fight (171) | 54 | 42 | 109 | 5 | 0 (inside a ZIP; temp extraction was cleaned up) |
| swoon (171) | 20 | 20 | 123 | 5 | 20 |
| trespass (171) | 44 | 44 | 274 | 11 | 44 |

**Caveats. These windows are not normal data until a human checks them.**
- **Few distinct scenes.** 506 nominal windows come from only **21 unique scenes**. Camera and season variants of the same staged scene are highly correlated, so the effective sample is closer to 21 than to 500.
- **Staged.** Actors are about to perform the event: walking toward the wall, approaching the gate, gathering. Many pre-event windows are hard-negative-like at best and pre-incident at worst.
- **Unverified boundaries.** AI Hub event and action intervals are not treated as exact visual truth in this project, which is why the 10 s guard is used. Each window needs a human "nothing operator-relevant happens" verification.
- **Domain.** Every window is 3840×2160 from a small set of AI Hub sites. That is not the camera mix of a real small-organization deployment.

**Use:** this is a cheap first source of *hard negatives* (loitering, walking near walls and gates, people close together). It is not enough for clear-normal coverage or for estimating real traffic.

## 4. Proposal: `477-triage-normal-v1` (separate extension; benchmark_v1 untouched)

**Purpose:** validate the NORMAL branch (what Stage 1 may suppress) and estimate the score distribution of benign traffic, so that escalation rate and cost become measurable.

### Composition (minimum viable)

| Group | Clips | Content | Source suggestion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clear normal | 30 | Normal walking, standing or sitting, people passing each other, an empty scene with a brief pass-through | A benign CCTV source (to acquire); a few 171 pre-event windows after human check |
| Hard negative | 40 | Crouching or bending then recovering; sitting down on the ground; normal running; normal doorway or gate entry with a key or badge; two people close together talking; brief non-conflict contact (handshake, hug); object pickup; camera occlusion or a dark scene with normal movement; crowd flow | 171 pre-event windows (human-verified), plus a benign source |
| New danger anchors | ≥ 20 | Human-anchored falls, conflicts and boundary crossings from **distinct** scenes | 171 / 71953 positives not used in v1 |
| **Total** | **~90** | | |

**Constraints:**
- No more than 3 clips per source scene, so correlated variants do not inflate n.
- Same frame protocol as v1 (`uniform_total64_v1`).
- Human-verified label for every clip: `clear_normal`, `hard_negative`, `danger` or `ambiguous`, with the observable fact written down.
- Labels and tiers frozen before any model run. Leakage audit as in P1–P4.

### Why these sizes
- With 0 failures in *n* trials, the 95% upper bound on the failure rate is about 3/*n* (rule of three).
  - 30 clear normals give a bound of about 10% on the clear-normal false-alert rate.
  - 40 hard negatives give about 7.5%.
  - 20 new anchors bound Danger→Normal at about 15%.
- These sizes can falsify the cascade or show it is plausible. They do not certify it. Production-level guarantees would need hundreds of anchors.

### Cost of running the extension (estimate from frozen means)
- **P1 on all ~90 clips:** about $0.04.
- **P4 on all ~90 clips:** about $37 (once, so Stage 2 can be measured on negatives too).
- **Human verification time** is the binding cost, not API spend.

### What it unlocks
These metrics become measurable: Safe-Normal Precision, False-Normal Rate on hard negatives, Stage 1 escalation rate on benign-heavy mixes, P4 NORMAL behavior on benign scenes, and a defensible T_normal and T_danger *range*.

A real traffic mix (benign:incident ratio and the arrival rate per camera) is still needed for monthly cost. Get it from a pilot site's Stage 0 candidate log, not from a benchmark.
